The Thesis

Silver and Sutton are right. The ceiling on imitation learning is fundamental, not a scaling problem. Intelligence that only recombines what humans produced cannot transcend it. The next leap requires agents that generate their own experience through action and consequence — not inherited knowledge, but discovered intelligence. I believe this is how it has to be done.
